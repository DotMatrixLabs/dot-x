Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Frogbyte AS and DotMatrixLabs Dot X ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares personal information when you use our application ("Licensed Application") and associated services. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal data is handled responsibly and in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including the European Union's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Information We Collect

    Personal Information: When you use our Licensed Application, we may collect certain personal information, such as your name, email address, and device identifiers. We only collect personal information that is necessary for the performance of our services.

    Usage Information: We may collect information about how you interact with our Licensed Application, such as the features you use, the settings you choose, and your interactions with other users.

    Technical Information: We may collect technical information about your device, such as its operating system, hardware model, and IP address.

    Telemetry in the Dot X desktop application: The application reports to two services by default. You can turn either service off at any time in Settings > General.

        Crash reports go to a Sentry instance that Frogbyte AS runs at sentry-ingest.frogbyte.io. The application sends a report only when it crashes or panics. Each report contains the error message that describes the failure, a stack trace, and the application version. A report does not contain a copy of the application's memory. An error message describes the operation that failed, and it can name the device, file, or value involved in that failure.

        Usage analytics go to Aptabase, in their European Union data region. The application reports when it starts, when it exits, an hourly signal that it is still running, when it detects a Decker device, and when you turn on MIDI output or open a MIDI port. Each of these carries the operating system name and version, the system locale, the webview engine and version, the application version, and a session identifier. The application generates the session identifier at random in memory on each launch and never writes it to disk. It does not derive the identifier from your device or your account, so the identifier cannot recognise you across launches.

        If you turn off a service in Settings > General, Dot X stops sending new reports or analytics events to that service.

        The analytics service does not receive your name, your email address, a lasting device identifier, file paths, the names of applications running on your machine, your channel or slider configuration, or any settings you have entered into plugins.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect for the following purposes:

    To provide and maintain our Application;
    To personalize your experience and improve our services;
    To analyze trends and usage patterns;
    To comply with legal obligations and protect our rights.

3. How We Share Your Information

We may share your personal information with third parties in the following circumstances:

    With service providers who help us operate our Licensed Application and provide related services;
    With legal authorities or regulatory bodies when required by law or to protect our rights;
    In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

4. Your Rights and Choices

    Access and Correction: You have the right to access and correct your personal information. You can do this by contacting us at post@frogbyte.io

    Data Portability: You have the right to request a copy of your personal information in a machine-readable format.

    Withdrawal of Consent: If you have provided consent for the processing of your personal information, you have the right to withdraw your consent at any time.

    Deletion: You have the right to request the deletion of your personal information, subject to certain limitations under applicable law.

5. Data Retention

We will retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

6. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. International Data Transfers

Your personal information may be transferred to and processed in countries outside of the European Economic Area (EEA) that may not provide the same level of data protection as your home country. In such cases, we will ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your personal information.

8. Updates to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the updated Privacy Policy on our website or through other communication channels.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at post@frogbyte.io.
