Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Frogbyte AS and DotMatrixLabs Dot X ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares personal information when you use our application ("Licensed Application") and associated services. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal data is handled responsibly and in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including the European Union's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Information We Collect

    Personal Information: When you use our Licensed Application, we may collect certain personal information, such as your name, email address, and device identifiers. We only collect personal information that is necessary for the performance of our services.

    Usage Information: We may collect information about how you interact with our Licensed Application, such as the features you use, the settings you choose, and your interactions with other users.

    Technical Information: We may collect technical information about your device, such as its operating system, hardware model, and IP address.

    What the Dot X desktop application actually sends: the desktop application reports to two services, both enabled by default. There is currently no setting to turn them off.

        Crash reports are sent to a Sentry instance operated by Frogbyte AS at sentry-ingest.frogbyte.io. These are sent only when the application crashes or panics, and contain the error message, a stack trace, the application version, and a minidump. A minidump is a snapshot of the application's memory at the moment it crashed. It necessarily includes the file paths of the program components that were loaded at the time, which on a per-user installation contain your operating system account name, and it may incidentally include other text the application was holding in memory, such as the names of audio sessions or applications running on your machine, labels you have given your channels, or values that were in transit to or from a plugin, which can include credentials you entered into a plugin's settings. We do not deliberately collect any of this, and we use it only to diagnose the crash, but we cannot list the contents of a memory snapshot exhaustively in advance.

        Usage analytics are sent to Aptabase, in their European Union data region. The application reports when it starts, when it exits, an hourly signal that it is still running, when a Decker device is detected, and when MIDI output is enabled or a MIDI port is opened. Each of these carries the operating system name and version, the system locale, the webview engine and version, the application version, and a session identifier. The session identifier is randomly generated in memory on every launch, is never written to disk, and is not derived from your device or your account, so it cannot be used to recognise you across launches.

        The analytics service does not receive your name, your email address, a persistent device identifier, file paths, the names of applications running on your machine, your channel or slider configuration, or any settings you have entered into plugins.
